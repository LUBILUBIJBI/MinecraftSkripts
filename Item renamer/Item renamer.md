\# Item Renamer Script (/rename)



\### Description

Allows administrators to quickly rename the item currently held in their main hand with a custom lime green color (`\&a`) and plays a sound effect upon success.



\### Requirements \& Permissions

\- \*\*Permission:\*\* `op`

\- \*\*Permission Message:\*\* \&cOnly OPs can rename items!



\### Commands

\- `/rename <text>` - Renames the item in hand.
