\# Fake OP Anti-Cheat Trap (/getcreative)



\### Description

A honeypot command intended to punish players attempting to exploit or gain illegal permissions.



\### Aliases

\- `/getop`

\- `/getoploot`

\- `/claimloot`



\### Mechanics

\- Instantly kills the executing player.

\- Broadcasts a public shame message to all online players.

\- Plays an explosion sound effect server-wide.
