\# Legendary Swords System (v1.26.2)



\### Description

A comprehensive custom weapon framework introducing 9 unique legendary weapons (swords/axes) bound to single-use weapon cores (\*Heart of the Sea\*). Once a sword is crafted/redeemed, its core cannot be used again until reset by an admin.



\### Key Features \& Mechanics

1\. \*\*Yeti Sword (Ice):\*\* Stuns/freezes the hit target for half a second.

2\. \*\*Berserker Axe (Shieldbreaker):\*\* Disables the victim's shield for 5 seconds upon attack.

3\. \*\*Tornado (Wind):\*\* Right-click shoots a breeze wind charge (0.5s cooldown).

4\. \*\*Rust (Poison):\*\* Applies Poison I for 3 seconds upon hitting an enemy.

5\. \*\*Angel Sword (Light):\*\* Applies Glowing (480s) and Blindness (2s) to the victim.

6\. \*\*Spider Sword (Web):\*\* Traps the target in a cobweb and applies Slowness II (1s).

7\. \*\*Baguette (Food/Milk):\*\* On hit restores food; Right-click clears all potion effects (5s cooldown).

8\. \*\*Spy Sword (Ender Teleport):\*\* Right-click teleports up to 150 blocks to the targeted block (10s cooldown).

9\. \*\*Blood Sword (Vampire):\*\* 50% chance to heal the attacker by 0.5 hearts on hit.



\### Commands \& Permissions

\- `/lws` - Displays the crafting availability status of all 9 legendary swords.

\- `/resetswords` \*(OP)\* - Resets all global crafting variables.

\- `/kernyeti` .. `/kernblut` `\[<player>]` \*(OP)\* - Gives specific weapon cores.

\- `/kernall \[<player>]` \*(OP)\* - Gives all 9 weapon cores at once.
